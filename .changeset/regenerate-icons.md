The icons on `@amerfathullah/myds-react/icon` are regenerated from their SVG sources by the repaired icon generator. Nothing you import or render changes: the entry exports the same names, each icon renders the same markup, and `MalaysiaFlagIcon` and `JataNegaraIcon` keep their deprecation notes. Only some icons' doc comments in the type declarations change, to name their export: `BookMOEIcon`, `MiniBookMOEIcon`, `PDFMediaIcon` and `RSSIcon`.
