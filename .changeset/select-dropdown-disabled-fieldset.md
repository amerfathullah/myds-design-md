Select and Dropdown no longer open from a press on a trigger that's disabled without their own `disabled` prop. Radix opens both on `pointerdown` and checks only that prop, but Chromium still sends a disabled button `pointerdown`. So inside a `<fieldset disabled>`, where the trigger looked disabled and Tab skipped it, a mouse press opened a Select or a Dropdown, and a touch tap opened a Dropdown. A pick then called `onValueChange` or an item's `onSelect`, and reached the form once the fieldset was enabled again. `<DropdownTrigger asChild><Button disabled>` opened the same way. SelectTrigger and DropdownTrigger now stop the press while their own button is `:disabled`, whatever disabled it: the prop, a disabled fieldset (plain, `group`, nested or disabled after mount, even with `disabled={false}` on the component), or `disabled` on an `asChild` child. A press on a disabled trigger also no longer moves focus. Before, a mouse press on a Dropdown trigger with `disabled`, or a touch tap on any trigger with `disabled`, moved it to the page's body. A trigger in the first `<legend>` of a disabled fieldset, which the browser leaves enabled, still opens. An `onPointerDown` you pass to either trigger, or to an `asChild` child, is still called on every press. Both triggers keep their prop types.

SelectLabel, the prefix a SelectValue `label` draws, now dims with the trigger it sits in. It took `txt-black-disabled` only from the `disabled` prop, so in a disabled fieldset it kept its enabled `txt-black-500` (`txt-black-900` in a `multiple` select) beside the dimmed value. An enabled Select's label also dimmed under any ancestor marked `group` with a `data-disabled` attribute, such as an open, disabled AccordionItem. It now follows the trigger's `:disabled`, as DateRangePicker's prefix does, and a label with a colour class of its own dims too. A `disabled` you added to each Select or DropdownTrigger in a disabled fieldset only to keep it shut, or to dim its label, can go. `full.css` in `@amerfathullah/myds-style` already carries the class, and doesn't change.
