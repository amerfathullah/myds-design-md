Applicant type

Date field and Time field

The focused-segment samples show what date-field-segment-active and time-field-segment-active ask for: primitive primary-500 with txt-white. Chromium and Firefox don't render it, so a real focused segment shows the browser's own highlight (a Defect). An invalid field shows the otl-danger-300 border and fr-danger ring only while unfocused; focus replaces them with otl-primary-300 and fr-primary. The default samples pass invalid={false}: without it the border is primitive gray-200 in both themes (a Defect). In a 12-hour locale at 1x, Chromium clips the AM/PM segment, as it does in the library (a Defect).
